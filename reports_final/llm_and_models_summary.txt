1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
XAI-StockGPT v2 is a modular, configuration-driven research framework that combines 
Predictive ML, Explainable AI (SHAP attributions), and Fine-Tuned Finance LLMs 
to generate faithful, natural-language trading signal rationales.

Pipeline Architecture:
  MARKET DATA (NSE / Yahoo Finance)
        ↓
  DATA PIPELINE (Cleaning, Validation, Deduplication)
        ↓
  FEATURE ENGINEERING (Trend, Momentum, Volatility, Volume, Returns)
        ↓
  CHRONOLOGICAL SPLITTING (Train / Validation / Locked Test)
        ↓
  PREDICTION MODELS (XGBoost / LightGBM / Random Forest / Neural Baseline)
        ↓
  SHAP EXPLANATIONS (TreeExplainer / Local Feature Attributions)
        ↓
  INSTRUCTION DATASET SYNTHESIS (Multi-Task JSONL Formatting)
        ↓
  FINANCE LLM FINE-TUNING (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct / LoRA PEFT)
        ↓
  FAITHFUL NATURAL-LANGUAGE RATIONALE GENERATION

4. LLM EVALUATION DIMENSIONS & GROUNDING CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evaluated across 9 scientific dimensions:

  2. SHAP Grounding Score: Ratio of top SHAP attributions present in output text

  9. Human Readability: Grammatical correctness and flow
